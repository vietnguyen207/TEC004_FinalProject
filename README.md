## ✨ Features by Role

### 🛡️ Admin
Admins have full operational and managerial access across the system:
* **User Management:** Add and manage students and instructors.
* **Course & Enrollment:** Create courses and process student enrollments.
* **Academic Control:** Modify student grades and compute student rankings.
* **Analytics & Reports:**
  * Access comprehensive system reports and visual charts.
  * Perform **GPA Analytics**.
  * Utilize **Performance Prediction Models** to identify high-performing and at-risk (low-performing) students.
* **Attendance & Data:**
  * Monitor and check attendance records.
  * Import and export data seamlessly via **JSON** and **CSV** files.

---

### 👨‍🏫 Instructor
Instructors manage their assigned courses, students, and classroom activities:
* **Course Overview:** View all assigned courses and class rosters.
* **Grading System:**
  * Customize grading criteria and percentage weightings.
  * Input and update student grades for assigned courses.
* **Assignment Management:** Full CRUD operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete) for course assignments.
* **Attendance:** Track and log classroom attendance.
* **Course Analytics:** Run performance prediction models to identify high and low-performing students within their specific courses.

---

### 👨‍🎓 Student
Students get a personalized portal to stay on top of their academic journey:
* **Academic Performance:** View current grades and GPA metrics.
* **Self Prediction:** Access personal grade prediction insights based on current performance.
* **Course & Attendance:** Check enrolled courses and view personal attendance logs.
* **Profile Management:** View and manage personal profile details.
